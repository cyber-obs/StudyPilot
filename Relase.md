## StudyPilot 1.0.0

First public release.

- Revision sessions start automatically when you open a note, with a timer, pause and a menu in the status bar.
- A short quiz ends each session. Questions come from your callouts, `question :: answer` cards (Spaced Repetition syntax) and `##` sections.
- Progress, last revision date and next revision date are written to the properties of each note.
- Missed questions come back sooner; known ones are spaced further apart.
- A tracking note shows progress per note, notes to review, your week and your streak.
- Optional integration with the Spaced Repetition plugin.
- Available in English, French and Spanish.

Install: copy `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/studypilot/`.
